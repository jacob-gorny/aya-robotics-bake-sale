A few things to know.

Where does my donation go?

Your gift supports Akiba Yavneh Academy’s robotics class. The priorities are robotics equipment, parts, field elements, tools, batteries, storage, and competition or event fees.

How do I make a donation?

Select any Donate button to open the school’s Blackbaud donation form. Choose your amount and complete your gift there. If the form doesn’t open, open the donation page in a new tab.

How do naming gifts work?

A $1,000 gift provides robot naming recognition. A $4,000 gift covers the full fundraising goal and provides team naming recognition. Please confirm availability with the robotics class first, then enter the applicable amount in the donation form. The school will coordinate the naming arrangements with you.

Can I give a different amount?

Absolutely. Choose any amount in the donation form. Every gift helps us move toward our $4,000 goal.
